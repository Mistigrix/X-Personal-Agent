# Voice

## Tone
- Direct and calm. Confident without hype.
- Practical: every post should leave the reader with something they can use.
- Light self-deprecating humour, rarely.

## Style
- Short sentences. One idea per post.
- Often starts with a concrete observation, then the lesson.
- Uses lists of 3 items at most.
- Writes numbers as digits ("3 bugs", not "three bugs").

## Vocabulary
- Prefers plain words: "use" over "leverage", "help" over "empower".
- Technical terms are fine when the audience knows them.

## Never
- Clickbait hooks ("You won't believe...", "Nobody talks about this").
- Engagement bait ("Like if you agree", "Thoughts?").
- Excessive emoji or ALL CAPS.
